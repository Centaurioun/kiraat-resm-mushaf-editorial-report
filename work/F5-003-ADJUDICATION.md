# F5-003 adjudication — positive formulation of the central thesis

## Locked Fifth Report item

F5-003 targets the Giriş sentence:

`Kitabın hareket noktası, resm-i Osmânî'nin kırâatleri meydana getiren bağımsız bir kaynak olmadığıdır.`

The stylistic defect is that the book's central thesis is introduced through a negative `değildir/olmadığıdır` formulation.

## Current durable context

In durable F5-002 P23 the target is immediately followed by a second sentence that already states the positive core in more precise Fourth-approved language:

`Çalışmada kırâatin aslî dayanağı, telakki ve müşâfehe yoluyla sürdürülen, edâ ve isnadla denetlenen rivâyet geleneği olarak kabul edilmektedir.`

The rest of P23 contains accepted Fourth-scientific qualifications distinguishing transmission, theoretical orthographic possibility, the joint acceptance criteria, and the role of resm-i Osmânî.

## Decision

Do not replace the whole paragraph with the shorter Fifth suggested paragraph. That would discard accepted Fourth material.

Do not simply insert the Fifth first sentence before the existing second sentence, because that would create immediate semantic duplication.

Instead replace the first **two** current sentences with one positive sentence that preserves the more precise existing terminology:

`Kırâatlerin aslî kaynağı, telakki ve müşâfehe yoluyla sürdürülen, edâ ve isnadla denetlenen rivâyet geleneğidir.`

All subsequent P23 content is preserved unchanged for its own later Fifth items.

This is the narrowest edit that:

- removes the F5-003 negative thesis opening;
- avoids duplicating the immediately following positive sentence;
- preserves the Fourth-approved scientific content and terminology;
- does not pre-apply later Fifth targets in the remainder of P23.
