# F5-004 adjudication — resm-i mushaf / resm-i Osmânî distinction

## Locked Fifth target

`Bu sebeple her mushaf yazısı resm-i Osmânî olmadığı gibi, resm-i mushaf ile resm-i Osmânî de bütün bağlamlarda birbirinin yerine kullanılamaz.`

The Fifth Report recommends replacing the negative contrast with a positive explanation of the two terms.

## Durable F5-003 context

Immediately before the target, P24 already defines both terms in accepted Fourth wording:

- `resm-i mushaf` is the field concerning the written features of Qur'anic words in mushafs;
- `resm-i Osmânî` is the historical writing tradition attributed to the mushafs copied in the period of Hz. Osman and followed through transmission in later resm literature.

Repeating the full Fifth suggested definition here would therefore duplicate the sentence immediately before it.

## Decision

Replace only the negative target sentence with:

`Bu iki terim, kapsamları farklı olduğu için bağlama göre ayrı kullanılmalıdır.`

This preserves the existing positive definitions, removes the negative `olmadığı gibi / kullanılamaz` construction, and avoids redundant redefinition.

No later P24 sentence is modified; F5-005+ remain untouched.
